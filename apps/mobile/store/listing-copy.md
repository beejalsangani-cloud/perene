## Description — max 4000 chars

> **Perene is your personal AI stylist — and it actually knows your closet.**
>
> Most style apps show you clothes you'll never buy. Perene is different. It
> learns the wardrobe you already own, then styles you from it — a real outfit for
> every day, not just for big events.
>
> **Get dressed in seconds, every morning**
> Open Perene and your looks for the day are already waiting — chosen for the
> weather, your plans, and your taste. Daytime and evening, ready to wear.
>
> **Your whole wardrobe, finally organized**
> Snap a photo of each piece and Perene tags it by color, fit, and vibe — so you
> stop forgetting what's hiding in the back of your closet. Everything, searchable,
> in your pocket.
>
> **Styled from what you own**
> No "buy these 12 things" lists. Perene builds outfits from your actual clothes,
> with a stylist's reasoning for why each look works.
>
> **A style profile that gets sharper over time**
> Tell Perene how you like to dress, and every suggestion tunes closer to *you* —
> your colors, your occasions, your budget. The more it learns, the better it gets.
>
> **Shop the gaps, smartly**
> When a look needs one more piece, Perene finds the right product from the right
> brands at the right price — no endless scrolling.
>
> **Find your next look**
> Browse a curated feed of inspiration and recreate any look with the clothes you
> already have.
>
> Perene is styling for your daily ritual — calm, personal, and genuinely yours.
>
> ---
>
> **Perene membership**
> Some features require a subscription. Your subscription unlocks daily outfit
> suggestions and more. Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation
> of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours
> before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel anytime in your App Store
> account settings.
>
> Terms of Use: https://myperene.com/terms
> Privacy Policy: https://myperene.com/privacy

*(~1,850 chars — well under the 4,000 cap. The subscription paragraph is required by
Apple review when the app has auto-renewing IAP; keep it.)*
